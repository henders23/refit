What we refit

Almost every small business is the same pipeline with a craft in the middle: capture what happened, turn it into a structured document, communicate it to a customer. We rebuild that pipeline around two named AI assistants, a prompt library and templates, trained on your real material.

Built for businesses of one to five people: designers, trades, therapists, tutors, photographers, accountants, celebrants, caterers.

Marketing

Every finished job harvested into posts, case studies and updates. A monthly batch written in one sitting, in your voice.

Operations

Site visits, consultations and job notes captured by voice or photo and turned into briefs, job sheets, quotes and schedules.

Customers

Same-day replies to enquiries, follow-up that actually happens, aftercare that brings people back, and reviews asked for at the right moment.

Billing

Proposals with staged fees and validity dates. A pipeline sheet that takes ten minutes a week. Nothing unquoted, unchased or undercharged.

How it works

Six stages, a defined output at each, and a check-in four weeks after handover that ends the engagement. Fixed fee, never hourly. Half on appointment, half at the third training session.

Stage 130 min, free

Qualify

One call to establish fit. Talk me through what you actually did yesterday, hour by hour. If I cannot see two recoverable hours a week, I will say so and not take the job.

OutputA go or no-go, and a fee quote.
Stage 290 min

Audit

Your last completed job walked through end to end, every document it generated, plus one real work episode recorded on your phone. We look for repetition, translation, leakage and friction.

OutputA workflow map: how it runs now and how it will run.
Stage 3Half a day

Design

The platform chosen by running your most common task live on both major ones and letting you feel the difference. Two assistants specified: one that catches your thinking, one that speaks in your voice.

OutputYour guide: the one document that describes the new way of working.
Stage 41 to 2 days

Build

Tone of voice captured first, from your own writing. Then the assistants, the prompt library and the templates, every one tested on your real material before training day.

OutputAssistants, prompt library, templates and a client questionnaire.
Stage 53 × 90 min

Train

Watch one, drive one, take one home. You are at the keyboard from minute twenty, and homework always runs on a live job with a real customer.

OutputA competent, confident user. By session three a real customer has been through the system.
Stage 630 min, then check-in

Handover

The playbook walked through, a weekly rhythm agreed, a check-in booked four weeks out. After the check-in, it is yours.

OutputA printed playbook: one card per workflow, kept by the kettle.

Independence, not dependency.

The engagement is designed so that you do not need us afterwards. Referrals come because we left.
