How it works

A short, structured engagement with a defined output at every stage.

Step 1

Tell us what you want to improve

A conversation about where the work is slow, repetitive or falling through the cracks. You set the priorities.

OutputA one-page brief naming the processes in scope and what better looks like.
Step 2

We map the process as it is

Who does what, in which tools, and where the time goes.

OutputA current-state map everyone in the business can read and correct.
Step 3

We redesign it together

Where AI genuinely helps, we put it in. Where it does not, we leave it out. You approve every change.

OutputThe new process, running, with your team trained on it.
Step 4

You own the result

Documentation, prompts, tools and accounts are handed over in full.

OutputA handover pack your team can run, change or extend without us.
